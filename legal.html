Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 10, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the ResellerBooks application ("Application"), owned and operated by Mazza Financial Systems LLC. ResellerBooks is a record-keeping utility designed specifically for e-commerce resellers to organize business records, expenses, and mileage.

2. Information Collection and Use

ResellerBooks is built with a "privacy-first" architecture. Most data remains local to your device or within your personal iCloud storage.

  • Bookkeeping Data: We process sales data, expense records, and mileage logs ("Audit-Proof Mileage Log") to provide core features.
  • On-Device Processing: Images of receipts or odometers are processed locally using Apple's on-device OCR. We do not send these images to external servers for processing.
  • Imported Data: We process data from CSV files and API connections (e.g., eBay) only to display and calculate records within the Application.

3. Advertising (Free Version Only)

The free version of the Application uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements.

  • IDFA Tracking: AdMob may use your Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) to provide personalized ads. The Application will request your permission via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.
  • Pro Tier: Users who upgrade to ResellerBooks Pro will have advertisements disabled, and the AdMob SDK will cease active tracking for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Integrations and Services

  • eBay Integration: We use OAuth 2.0 for secure data syncing. Your credentials are never shared with or stored by the Application.
  • Apple Services: We utilize iCloud/CloudKit for data syncing and StoreKit 2 for managing Pro subscriptions and billing.
  • Location Services: Address lookups for mileage tracking utilize Apple MapKit. No background GPS tracking is performed.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You control your data. You may delete individual entries or use the "Delete All Data" feature in settings. To remove data synced to iCloud, you must manage your storage via your Apple Account settings.

If you have questions about privacy, contact: resellerbookssupport@gmail.com
