Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 10, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the ResellerBooks mobile application ("Application"), owned and operated by Mazza Financial Systems LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). ResellerBooks is a record-keeping utility designed for e-commerce resellers to organize sales, expenses, and mileage for bookkeeping purposes.

2. Privacy-First Architecture

ResellerBooks is built so that your financial data stays on your device and, if enabled, syncs privately through your personal iCloud account. We do not operate application servers that store your data. We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry of any kind.

3. Information We Process

  • Bookkeeping Data: The Application processes sales transactions, expense records, and mileage trip logs stored via Apple's SwiftData framework and synced via CloudKit.
  • Platform Preferences: Marketplaces you select during onboarding (e.g., eBay, Poshmark, Mercari) are stored locally to tailor the interface.
  • Photos: Receipt and odometer photos are stored on-device and in your private iCloud. They are never uploaded to our servers.
  • On-Device OCR: Text extraction from photos is performed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework.
  • Cross-Device Settings: Non-sensitive preferences (timezone, display mode) are synced via Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store.

4. Third-Party Services

  • eBay Integration: Uses OAuth 2.0. A transient Vercel-hosted bridge facilitates the code exchange but does not store credentials or transaction data.
  • Google AdMob (Free Tier Only): Used to display advertisements. May use your IDFA for personalization following an ATT prompt. AdMob is disabled for Pro users.
  • Apple Services: Includes iCloud/CloudKit for sync, StoreKit 2 for billing, and MapKit/CoreLocation for distance and geocoding (no background GPS tracking).
  • CSV Import: All parsing of exported files (from eBay, Amazon, etc.) happens entirely on your device.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect analytics, telemetry, or continuous location data. We do not store your data on our servers or sell your information to third parties.

6. Data Export

You may export all data (records and photos) as a ZIP archive containing CSV files. This is generated locally on your device.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You control your data. Records can be deleted individually or via "Delete All Data" in Settings. iCloud data must be managed via your Apple Account settings.
