Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 17, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the ResellerBooks application operated by Mazza Financial Systems LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). ResellerBooks is a bookkeeping and organization tool for resellers that helps you track sales, expenses, mileage, receipts, and related tax records.

2. Data Handling Model

ResellerBooks is designed to keep your data under your control. App data is stored on your device and may sync through your private iCloud / CloudKit container when available. We do not operate a general-purpose backend that stores your bookkeeping records, photos, or mileage logs.

When CloudKit is unavailable, the app may temporarily operate in local-only mode until iCloud access is restored. Local-only storage is an operational fallback, not a separate data product.

3. Information The App Processes

  • Sales and expense data: Transaction details, cost records, and summaries you enter or import.
  • Mileage data: Trip logs, odometer entries, and deduction calculations used by the mileage tools.
  • Receipt and photo data: Images captured in-app or selected from your library for receipts, mileage verification, or related records.
  • OCR text: On-device text extraction from photos to help populate fields and reduce manual entry.
  • Settings and preferences: Non-sensitive preferences such as display choices, sync settings, and bookkeeping defaults.
  • Subscription state: Pro entitlement and billing status handled through Apple’s StoreKit.
  • Ad-related state: The app includes sponsored ad surfaces and related presentation logic in free or eligible builds.

4. Storage And Sync

Bookkeeping records, mileage logs, and receipt photos may be stored locally and synchronized with your private iCloud account when CloudKit is available. Some preference data may also sync through Apple services so your configuration stays consistent across devices.

You can export or delete your data through app settings. Depending on your Apple account and iCloud status, some data may remain in your private Apple storage until removed through the appropriate Apple or app controls.

5. OCR And Camera Use

The app uses device capabilities to capture photos of receipts and odometer readings and to process those images with on-device OCR. OCR results are used to help you enter information more quickly and are not a substitute for reviewing the underlying image or source document.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Apple: Used for iCloud / CloudKit, StoreKit subscriptions, photos, camera access, and platform features.
  • Google AdMob and related ad tooling: Used for sponsored ad surfaces and ad delivery where enabled. Ad behavior may vary by build, region, and entitlement state.
  • External toolkit links: The Toolkit section may open third-party websites such as TurboTax, Vendoo, Amazon, or other merchant links. Those sites have their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not claim to collect continuous location data, sell personal information, or run analytics that track your bookkeeping activity across unrelated services. If third-party services are used, their own policies may apply to data they collect independently of ResellerBooks.

8. Data Export And Deletion

You may export your records through the app’s export tools. You may also delete app data from within ResellerBooks. If data is stored in your private iCloud container, Apple account settings and iCloud storage controls may also be relevant.
