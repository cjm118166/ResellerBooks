Everything a reseller actually needs

The app is built around the tasks that matter daily: logging sales, categorizing expenses, capturing receipts, tracking mileage, and keeping your records synced and exportable.

Sales, expenses, and profit tracking

Keep your marketplace activity organized in one ledger. ResellerBooks is designed for ongoing bookkeeping, not just one-time imports, so you can understand what you earned and what it cost.

Mileage tools that are built in

Log trips, verify odometer readings, and keep mileage deduction data ready for tax season. The Pro tier centers on this workflow and removes ads from the app.

OCR receipt capture

Use the camera or photo library to pull text from receipts and reduce repetitive typing. OCR happens on-device for speed and privacy.

Private iCloud sync

Your records can sync through iCloud / CloudKit across devices signed into your Apple account, with local-only fallback when iCloud is unavailable.

Exports for tax prep and audit trails

Build export packages from the app so you can share bookkeeping summaries, audit data, or CSV records with a CPA or keep them for your own records.

Export tools that save time

Generate exports for bookkeeping, audit support, and tax prep without rebuilding your records by hand.

Why people can trust it

ResellerBooks is built around privacy, transparency, and dependable bookkeeping outcomes.

Data control

Records are designed to stay on your device and in your private Apple storage, not in a general-purpose vendor backend.

Honest subscription model

Pro is a straightforward StoreKit subscription with Apple-managed billing, renewals, and cancellations.

Clear feature boundaries

External links are explicit

Frequently asked questions

What does ResellerBooks actually do?

It gives resellers a single place to track sales, expenses, mileage, receipt photos, and exports so bookkeeping is easier than maintaining spreadsheets across multiple platforms.

Which platforms does it support?

The app is aimed at sellers using eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Whatnot, Etsy, Facebook Marketplace, Amazon, Walmart, and similar resale channels.

Is my data private?

Yes. The app is designed for local storage and private iCloud / CloudKit sync when available. It does not depend on a generic hosted customer database for your bookkeeping records.

What about ads?

Free usage may include sponsored placements. ResellerBooks Pro centers the experience on exports, mileage, and bookkeeping tools without ad surfaces.
