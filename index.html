Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track eBay and Poshmark taxes?

ResellerBooks allows you to import your CSV reports directly from eBay and Poshmark into a master ledger. It automatically calculates gross sales, fees, shipping, and refunds to give you a tax-ready net profit dashboard.

How do I format a master ledger for reselling?

A master ledger should include Date, Platform, Order ID, Item Title, Gross Sales, Fees, Shipping, Refunds, and Adjustments. Enter costs as positive numbers and credits as negative numbers. You can download our official Google Sheets template above to get started instantly.

Can I export my data for my CPA?

Yes. You can generate a comprehensive PDF Report or export your entire database as an Audit Zip file directly from the app.
